About Me

I'm a small business owner. I run a printing shop that makes tarpaulins, decal stickers and more, and I also have a small tapsihan. I own a Pisonet shop with 8 units and operate a Bajaj here in Antipolo. I rent out retractable tents too, and I sell on online platforms like Shopee, Lazada and TikTok.

I'm also a student right now, and my dream is to land a job in the corporate world.

I troubleshoot and maintain all the computers in my Pisonet shop myself, so hardware is something I work with every day.
